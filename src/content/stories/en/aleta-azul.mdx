<DropCap>A paper cup passes through an opening cut into the wall and the coffee lands in your hand, no counter in between. That is how Aletazul x Folka introduces itself.</DropCap>

Aletazul is a Mexican technical outerwear brand: quick-dry fabrics, bamboo fiber, garments built for the water and worn every day. It runs eight stores in the country. The one in the Monterrey area sits at Río Mississippi 480, in San Pedro Garza García. It is not a café, which is exactly why this works.
