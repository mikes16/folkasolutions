<DropCap>There is a collaboration underway between Folka and Aletazul. For now, this teaser is the only part of it out in public.</DropCap>

Aletazul is a Mexican technical outerwear brand: quick-dry fabrics, bamboo fiber, garments built for the water and worn every day. It runs eight stores in the country. The one in the Monterrey area sits at Río Mississippi 480, in San Pedro Garza García. It is not a café, which is exactly why the piece works.

In the video the coffee comes through a hole cut into the wall: a paper cup with latte art passes from one hand to another through the opening, no counter in between. On the microcement bar, where ALETAZUL is spelled out, there are cookies. Behind it, folded tees and hanging hoodies. The piece closes on a single line: ALETAZUL X FOLKA.

The full story comes later. When it does, it lands right here: what leads a clothing brand to serve coffee on its retail floor, and how you build a bar inside a space that was never designed for one. For now, the teaser.
